## Problem Statement
Students often make many small daily payments for food, transport, stationery, entertainment and other needs. Without a simple record, it becomes difficult to understand where money is being spent and whether monthly spending is within a planned budget.

## Approach
Build a modular console application that:
1. Captures expenses through a simple menu
2. Validates all inputs before saving
3. Stores data in local CSV/JSON files (no database)
4. Generates monthly and category-wise spending reports against a budget

## Scope
The project provides a local console application for recording expenses, deleting incorrect records, setting a monthly budget and viewing monthly/category-wise spending summaries. It is designed for individual use on a personal computer.

## Target Users
Students and individual users who want a simple offline expense-recording tool without installing or managing a database server.

## High-Level Features
- Expense entry
- Expense listing
- Expense deletion
- Monthly budget management
- Monthly spending report
- Category-wise analysis
- Input validation
- Local file persistence

## Findings (Summary)
- File-based storage is enough for a single-user personal tracker.
- Validation before save keeps data reliable.
- Category reports make spending patterns easy to see.
- Modular design improves clarity for both coding and evaluation.
